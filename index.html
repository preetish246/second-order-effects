Second Order Effects
The first consequence is easy to see, I write about the ones after that

Every headline event — a war, a rate decision, a missile fired at a container ship — has an obvious, first-order consequence. This is a small notebook about the second one: the fuel bill, the market crash, the rerouted parcel, the rupee that quietly loses ten percent of its value while everyone is watching something else. I started keeping these notes to make sense of things I was reading about anyway, and this is where I've put them in order.
